How to use SauceDemo

A demo e-commerce site for practice and testing. Here's everything you need to log in, browse, and complete a purchase.

Logging in

1
Go to the site

Open saucedemo.com in your browser. You'll land directly on the login page.

2
Enter your username

Type standard_user in the Username field.

3
Enter your password

Type secret_sauce in the Password field.

4
Click Login

You'll be taken to the product listing page. If you see a red error message, check that there are no typos in your credentials.

You'll stay logged in for the duration of your browser session. Closing the tab or clicking the burger menu → Reset App State will log you out.

Browsing and adding to cart

1
Browse the products

After logging in you'll see 6 products. Use the sort dropdown (top right) to order them by name or price.

2
Add an item to your cart

Click the Add to cart button below any product. The cart icon in the top right will show a red number — that's your item count.

3
View or remove items

Click the cart icon to see everything you've added. To remove an item, click the Remove button next to it.

Clicking a product name or image opens its detail page. You can add it to the cart from there too.

Checking out

1
Open your cart

Click the cart icon in the top right. Review your items, then click Checkout.

2
Fill in your details

Enter a first name, last name, and postal code. You can use any values — this is a demo site, no real data is required.

3
Review your order

Check the order summary — you'll see each item, the item total, tax, and the final total. Click Finish when ready.

4
Order confirmed

You'll see a confirmation page with "Thank you for your order!" Your cart is now empty and you can start again.

No real payment is processed. SauceDemo is a test environment — entering real card details is unnecessary and not recommended.

Common questions

Are there other usernames I can use?
Yes. SauceDemo provides several test accounts, each with different behaviour:
Username What it does
standard_user Normal Full access, everything works
locked_out_user Blocked Cannot log in — shows an error
problem_user Buggy Images are broken, some buttons misbehave
performance_glitch_user Slow Login and page loads are deliberately delayed
error_user Errors Some actions trigger error messages
visual_user Visual bugs Layout issues on certain pages
All accounts use the same password: secret_sauce
I typed the right password but it still says "Username and password do not match"
Double-check for extra spaces before or after the username or password — they are easy to add accidentally. Copy-pasting from a document can sometimes include hidden characters. Try typing the credentials manually instead.
Can I go back to the product list while in checkout?
Yes. During checkout step 1 (entering your details) you can click Cancel to return to the cart. On the order summary page (step 2) you can also click Cancel to go back. Once you click Finish, the order is complete and cannot be undone — but you can simply start a new one.
The product images all look wrong or broken
This is expected if you're logged in as problem_user. That account is intentionally set up with broken images to simulate a buggy application. Log out and log back in as standard_user to see the correct images.
How do I log out?
Click the burger menu icon (three horizontal lines) in the top left corner, then click Logout. You'll be taken back to the login page.
My cart has items from a previous session — how do I clear it?
Open the burger menu (top left) and click Reset App State. This clears your cart and returns the product buttons to "Add to cart". It does not log you out.
